About us

Built for Somerset’s young athletes

Our mission

Our mission is to provide high level developmental football and cheer programs.

Where we play

Somerset Little Eagle Football participates in the Laurel Highlands Developmental Football League (LHDFL). We have tackle football teams at grade levels K–6 with a season beginning in August and ending in October.

Volunteers

We are an all-volunteer organization, and we appreciate everyone who steps up to make the season work for all involved. Get in touch if you would like to help.

  • All-volunteer Run by volunteers who meet monthly throughout the year.
  • Cleared coaches Every coach must hold recent child abuse and criminal background clearances and complete concussion awareness training.
  • Safe and positive A safe, positive environment for every participant.

Player expectations

Players and families should know what the season asks of them.

Expectation of all players
  • Arrival to practices and games on time.
  • Consistent attendance of scheduled games and practices.
  • Put forth a reasonable effort at both practices and games.
  • Display respectful behavior toward the coaches and teammates at all times.

Somerset Little Eagle Football, along with the coaching staff, reserves the right to reduce playing time or ask a player not to participate if any of the above expectations are not met.

Code of conduct

A safe and fun learning environment for our youth football players and cheerleaders is the expectation for everyone involved.

Read the code of conduct

The Somerset Little Eagle Football organization strives to provide a safe and fun learning environment for our youth football players and cheerleaders. Our expectation is that all involved will abide by the code of conduct and recognize that this is not an exhaustive list. Violations of this code of conduct or any act deemed unbecoming shall be investigated by the executive board and penalties may be imposed.
